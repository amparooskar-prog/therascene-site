01 / Occupational Therapy

Everyday skills,
step by step.

Occupational therapy focuses on helping people take part in the everyday activities that matter to them. For children, that may include routines at home, learning, play, and participating in daily life.

Ask about occupational therapy ↗
Little steps add up.

What occupational therapy can support

Skills for real-life
moments.

Occupational therapy (OT) uses meaningful everyday activities to support participation in daily living. The specific focus depends on the child’s needs and goals; the examples below describe common areas of OT, not a confirmed list of TheraScene session activities.

✳

Daily routines

Practicing self-care tasks such as dressing, eating, or getting ready for the day.

✎

Learning & play

Taking part in activities like using classroom tools, drawing, writing, playing, and organizing materials.

↗

Participation

Finding practical ways to join routines and activities at home, school, and in the community.

A good first conversation

Start with what
matters at home.

Share the everyday activities your child enjoys, finds tricky, or wants to do more independently. Ask the center how it learns about a child’s priorities and how families are included in setting goals.

Availability, assessment steps, session format, and individual goals should be confirmed directly with TheraScene.
